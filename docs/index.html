What does the bot monitor?

The upstream tracks unusual private-jet activity over a rolling 24-hour window and assigns an emergency level from 1 to 5. This bot checks every 30 minutes and reports changes in either direction. Occasional service messages and reminders are separate from alerts.

Is this an official emergency service?

No. This free, independent bot relays aircraft-activity data. It does not predict disasters or replace official emergency guidance. Screenshots are examples, not live readings.

How do I stop alerts?

Run /unsubscribe in each subscribed channel or DM, or send unsubscribe to the bot in a DM. You can also remove the app from Discord. See the terms of use and privacy policy.
