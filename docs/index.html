What does the bot monitor?

The upstream tracks unusual private-jet activity over 24 hours and assigns a level from 1 to 5. This bot checks every 30 minutes and reports increases and decreases.

Is this an official emergency service?

No. This free, independent bot relays aircraft data, not disaster predictions. Follow official emergency guidance.

How do I stop alerts?

/unsubscribe stops alerts in that channel or DM. You can also DM unsubscribe or remove the app.
