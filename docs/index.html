// more

The upstream tracks unusual private-jet activity over a rolling 24-hour window and assigns an emergency level from 1 to 5. This bot polls the upstream every 30 minutes and announces every transition — both rising and falling. Most days the level stays at 1 and you hear nothing.

// alert ladder

2 ↑   Notice. Emergency level 2.
3 ↑   ELEVATED. Emergency level 3.
4 ↑   WARNING. Emergency level 4.
5 ↑   ATTENTION. EMERGENCY LEVEL 5.
↓     Stand-down. Emergency level returned to N.

Each transition message also carries the upstream's text label, the z-score, and an asOf timestamp. On a real level-5 event you'll additionally receive the full incident payload (title, link, pubDate) from the upstream RSS feed.

// level-5 incident payload

ATTENTION. EMERGENCY LEVEL 5.
{title}
{pubDate}
{link}
